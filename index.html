About us

Meridian Clinic is the short public form of Meridian Integrated Health & Rehabilitation Inc., a psychology and psychotherapy clinic serving Richmond Hill and, online, communities across Ontario. Our work is grounded in respectful, evidence-informed care — simple, human, and centred around each person who comes to us.

Our approach

Getting started is kept simple, and each step is explained as we go.

1

Book

Contact us through this site, by phone, or on WhatsApp to arrange a first appointment.

2

Meet your therapist

Sessions are held at our office or online, depending on what suits you best.

3

Continue at your pace

Together, you and your therapist agree on the focus and pace of your work.

Our therapists work in English and Persian. Couples work at the clinic draws on Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT). Your privacy is respected throughout, and online sessions are available when coming in person is difficult.

Services

The services below reflect the care offered at the clinic. Details of any service can be discussed with our team before you book.

Individual Therapy

One-to-one sessions for adults, focused on the concerns you bring.

Couples Therapy

Sessions for partners who want support working through difficulties together, drawing on EFT.

Child & Adolescent Therapy

Care for children and teenagers, with parents involved as appropriate.

Family Therapy

Sessions with family members together, to support communication at home.

Motor Vehicle Accident (MVA) Support

Care following a motor vehicle accident, with support for FSRA and insurance forms and claims.

Psychological Assessment

Assessment services, arranged and explained in advance by our team.

Online Therapy

Sessions by Google Meet, Zoom or WhatsApp, wherever you are in Ontario.

Practicum Placement & Supervision

Supervised practicum placements for students, offered in partnership with Yorkville University.

Common questions

What languages do your therapists speak?

Therapy is offered in English and Persian, and some of our therapists are bilingual.

How do I book an appointment?

Through the booking page on this website, by phone at 905-650-6800, or through our support on WhatsApp. We will confirm a time that works for you.

How do I choose a therapist?

You can read about our therapists, or contact our support team and we will help you find a suitable match.

Can sessions be held online?

Yes. Online sessions are held by Google Meet, Zoom or WhatsApp. In-person sessions are held at our office.

Can I stay anonymous?

Your privacy is respected. Please talk with our team about any concern you have before your first session.

Do your therapists prescribe medication?

No. Our therapists provide psychotherapy and do not prescribe medication.

What do sessions cost?

Please contact us for current fees, and we will explain the options clearly before you book.
